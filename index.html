Intro

Forging reliable gameplay systems, rapid prototypes, and data-driven workflows to empower the whole team.

I'm a Technical Game Designer based in France with 4 years of experience shipping PC and console titles in small, multidisciplinary teams. I design and implement gameplay systems with a focus on durability and team usability: building data-driven architectures, clear documentation, and workflows that let the whole team iterate safely and efficiently. From early prototype to post-launch updates, I stay involved to make sure the systems I build remain solid, adaptable, and easy to evolve.
